# Self-Updating Portfolio

A portfolio website that keeps itself fresh. Your GitHub repos update on their own, and your LinkedIn posts appear automatically. You never touch code.

**[View the live demo](https://self-updating-portfolio.vercel.app)**

## Get yours in 3 steps — no technical skills needed

1. **Copy** this link: `https://github.com/aakash1998/self-updating-portfolio`
2. **Paste** it into Muse (or any AI assistant) and say: *"make this portfolio mine"*
3. **Answer 5 short questions.** Your site goes live.

That's it. The AI reads your LinkedIn, picks your best GitHub repos, fills in the site, and deploys it.

## What you get

- A clean, professional one-page portfolio
- **GitHub section that updates itself** — your featured repos always show live descriptions and activity
- **Writing section fed by your LinkedIn posts** — publish on LinkedIn, it appears on your site
- A services section and booking button if you take clients
- Free hosting, your own repo, your own domain whenever you want one

## How it stays updated

Everything on the site renders from one file: `data/site.json`. Your AI assistant edits that file whenever something changes — new role, new repos, new email. The site rebuilds itself on every update. If you ever want to edit it by hand, it's the only file you need to open.
